Free, ad-free daily planner

Every feature is free, with no ads. Keep tasks, habits and countdowns together in Kind To-Do. No account or in-app purchases required.

Tasks: Add items directly in your list. Set importance, dates, notes, repeats and reminders, and revisit completed tasks. Check today's habits from the task list too.

Habits: Fill one pixel a day. See 7 days, 14 days, a month, 6 months or a year at a glance. Choose scheduled weekdays and pixel colors, and edit past records.

Countdowns: Save trips, exams and anniversaries. See days remaining or elapsed, and pin important dates to the top.

Widgets: See 7 days, a month or a year of habit records on your Home Screen. Choose a habit for each widget.

Make it yours: Choose from 8 completion sounds, random playback and 3 celebration effects. Turn feedback off or change the app's opening screen.

Records stay on your device. Export and import backup files yourself. Accounts and automatic cross-device sync are not supported. Back up before uninstalling or changing devices.

Privacy policy

Kind To-Do has no accounts, ads, tracking or analytics services. The developer does not collect or transmit your tasks, habits or countdown records to a server. Records and preferences are stored on your device.

Notification permission is used only for task reminders you set. File selection and sharing are used only for backup imports and exports you request. Exported files follow the policies of the storage location or app you choose.

Reviews use Apple's or Google's official features. If you email support, your message and email address are used only to respond to your inquiry. Do not send sensitive records or passwords.

Uninstalling removes records stored in the app. Delete separately exported backup files yourself. Contact the email below for privacy questions or deletion requests.
